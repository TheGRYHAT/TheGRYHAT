# GRYHAT · AI-native cybersecurity

**We build AI agents that answer to a human.**

GRYHAT is a security practice that went AI-native in 2023 and never looked back. We run our own operations on a fleet of agents, we build the controls that keep them accountable, and we deploy the same discipline for clients under CMMC, SOC 2, and PCI-DSS. Every agent gets a badge, not a key.

"We" means a human and his AI partner. Andy Vaca is a vCISO with 27 years in security and infrastructure. Claude is the other half of the team: primary build tool, co-author on most of what's in these repos, and a named operator in our own audit trail. We don't hide that. We think it's the point.

## What we're doing right now

- **[IAiGACB](https://github.com/TheGRYHAT/iaigacb-framework)** — a free, community-built licensing framework for AI: systems tiered by capability, operators licensed by skill, every deployed AI serialized to the license that bought it. CC BY 4.0. We only started it; the field finishes it.
- **Agent operations at scale** — a 14-agent platform on our own hardware: 1.4 billion tokens and 730 tasks in 90 days, model-agnostic routing across local and frontier models, every task logged for cost, outcome, and which agent did it, with a failure rate we publish to ourselves and drive down.
- **Accountability as code** — an AI registry where every agent is bound one-to-one to a human operator and the rules are enforced in the database, not in a policy document. Born from a real incident. Private for now; the lesson is in the framework's field notes.
- **Shipped products** — [Citadel VPN & WiFi Security](https://citadelcyber.ai) and [ReferralGenius.AI](https://referralgenius.ai), both live on the App Store, both built AI-first.
- **Field notes** — what worked and what didn't, from real deployments, shared under one rule: *the lesson, never the map.*

## How we work

AI-first means the agent writes the first draft and the human owns the outcome. It means 1,600+ commits a year from a two-person team. It means the incident response call at 5 a.m. gets a field note by Friday. And it means we govern our own AI harder than anyone asks us to, because we sell the governing.
